Before coming to Oxford, I studied German at Beijing Foreign Studies University. At the time, my interests were mainly in phonetics and phonology. In 2023, I had the opportunity to spend a year at the University of Göttingen as an exchange student. There, I took courses in phonology, syntax and *Mediävistik*. Towards the end of that academic year, I also attended the 9th Naxos Summer School on Diachronic Linguistics.
